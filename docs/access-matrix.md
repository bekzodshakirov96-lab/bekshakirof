# Rollar va ruxsatlar matritsasi

| Modul | Rahbar (admin) | Buxgalter (accountant) | Oddiy foydalanuvchi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard va hisobotlar | Ko‘rish | Ko‘rish | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Agentlar | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ko‘rish | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Mijozlar | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ko‘rish | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Mahsulotlar | Ko‘rish, yaratish, narxni yangilash | Ko‘rish, yaratish, narxni yangilash | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Savdo va to‘lov jurnali | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Kassa jurnali | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ko‘rish, yaratish, tahrirlash | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Excel import | Yuklash va tarixni ko‘rish | Yuklash va tarixni ko‘rish | Ruxsat yo‘q |
| Foydalanuvchi rollari | Buxgalter rolini berish yoki bekor qilish | Ruxsat yo‘q | Ruxsat yo‘q |

Backendda biznes ko‘rish va moliyaviy amallar `businessProcedure`, faqat rahbarga tegishli strukturaviy hamda rol amallari esa `ownerProcedure` orqali himoyalangan.
